Frequently Asked Questions

  • How often does wind data update?
    Weir Wood Reservoir updates every 5 minutes, Worthing Pier every 10 minutes, and Bough Beech Reservoir every 15 minutes.
  • What do the emoji indicators mean?
    Each emoji represents a wind speed range from calm to too strong. You can customise the thresholds in Settings.
  • Why does the data show a warning overlay?
    A stale data warning appears when a reading hasn't been updated within the expected interval, which may indicate a temporary issue with the data source.
  • What does the orange tint on the tide card mean?
    It indicates that the current time is within the high tide window. The window size is configurable in Settings (default is 2 hours either side of high tide).
  • Where does the data come from?
    Worthing Pier data comes from the Channel Coastal Observatory, Weir Wood from an Ecowitt weather station, Bough Beech from a Weather.com personal weather station, and tides from tidetimes.org.uk.
