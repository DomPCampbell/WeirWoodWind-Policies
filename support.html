Frequently Asked Questions

How often does wind data update?

Weir Wood Reservoir updates every 5 minutes, Worthing Pier every 10 minutes, and Bough Beech Reservoir every 15 minutes.

What do the emoji indicators mean?

Each emoji represents a wind speed range, from calm to too strong. You can customise the thresholds in Settings to match your preferred activity.

Why does a card show a stale data warning?

A stale data warning appears when a reading hasn't been updated within the expected interval. This usually indicates a temporary issue with the data source and will resolve on its own.

What does the orange tint on the tide card mean?

It indicates that the current time is within the high tide window. The window size is configurable in Settings (default is 2 hours either side of high tide).

Where does the data come from?

Worthing Pier data comes from the Channel Coastal Observatory, Weir Wood from an Ecowitt weather station, Bough Beech from a Weather.com personal weather station, and tides from tidetimes.org.uk.

Can I change the wind speed units?

Yes. Open Settings and choose between mph, knots, or kph. The change applies across all locations immediately.

How do I reset my settings?

In Settings, use the reset button to restore the default wind speed thresholds. You can also uninstall and reinstall the app to clear all preferences.

How do I connect my Strava account?

Open any location's detail screen and tap the Strava button, or go to Settings and tap Connect Strava. You'll be taken to Strava's login page to authorise the app — WeirWoodWind never sees your Strava password.

Which Strava activities does the app show?

Only windsurf and kitesurf activities from the last 30 days are shown. Other activity types are filtered out automatically.

How do I disconnect Strava?

Go to Settings and tap Disconnect Strava. This removes your Strava tokens from the app immediately. You can also revoke access directly from your Strava account settings at strava.com/settings/apps.

Data Sources

  • Weather.com (Bough Beech Reservoir)
