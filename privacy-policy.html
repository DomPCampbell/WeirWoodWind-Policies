WeirWoodWind

Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect your data

Effective date: 5 June 2026

1 Introduction

WeirWoodWind ("the App") is a personal tool for checking wind and tide conditions at watersports locations in the UK. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is stored, and your rights regarding that information. By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2 Information We Collect

WeirWoodWind does not require any account or login. The core wind and tide features work entirely without providing any personal information. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

If you choose to connect your Strava account (see section 6), your Strava OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device as described below.

3 Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores your preferences locally on your device, including:

  • Wind speed unit (mph, knots, or kph)
  • Wind indicator thresholds
  • High tide window setting
  • Disclaimer acceptance flag
  • What's New version seen flag

If you connect Strava, your Strava OAuth access and refresh tokens are also stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

4 Data Retention and Deletion

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted to us, there is nothing retained on our end. Local preferences stored on your device can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage in your device settings.

To remove your Strava connection specifically, go to Settings and tap Disconnect Strava. This immediately deletes your Strava tokens from your device.

5 Third-Party Data Sources

The App fetches publicly available wind and tide data from the following sources:

  • Channel Coastal Observatory (Worthing Pier)
  • Ecowitt (Weir Wood Reservoir)
  • Weather.com (Bough Beech Reservoir)
  • tidetimes.org.uk (Shoreham tide times)

These requests are made directly from your device. The App does not act as an intermediary and does not log or store any network traffic. Please refer to each provider's own privacy policy for details on how they handle incoming requests.

6 Strava Integration (Optional)

Connecting Strava is entirely optional. All wind and tide features work without it. You can connect or disconnect at any time from the Settings screen.

When you tap Connect Strava, you are taken to Strava's own login page to authorise the App. WeirWoodWind never sees your Strava username or password — the OAuth process is handled entirely by Strava.

Once connected, the App stores an OAuth access token and refresh token locally on your device. These tokens allow the App to fetch your recent Strava activities on your behalf.

What the App fetches from Strava:

  • A list of your recent windsurf and kitesurf activities (last 30 days)
  • For a selected activity: name, date, duration, distance, and GPS speed data

This data is used only to display session information and overlay GPS speed on the wind graphs within the App. It is not transmitted to WeirWoodWind servers, not stored beyond the current session, and not shared with any third party.

Strava's own privacy policy applies to data Strava holds about you.

WeirWoodWind accesses your Strava data only with your explicit authorisation. You can revoke access at any time from Settings → Disconnect Strava, or directly via your Strava account settings.

7 Third-Party Sharing

WeirWoodWind does not share, sell, rent, or disclose any of your data to third parties. The only outbound connections the App makes are:

  • Direct reads from the wind and tide data sources listed in section 5
  • Strava API calls, when you have connected your Strava account

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

8 Your Rights

As the App does not collect any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data held by us to access, modify, or delete. You can remove all locally stored preferences at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage.

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, including the right to access, rectification, and erasure of your personal data. Since all data is stored locally and not processed by us, these rights are effectively exercised through the controls described above.

9 Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, please contact us so we can advise on how to remove the data from the device.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
